ADD WEATHER & RIVERS TO YOUR HOME SCREEN

Android controls widget placement, so you may need to add it from your launcher rather than from inside the app.

1
Return to your phone's home screen

Leave Weather & Rivers installed. You don't need to uninstall or reinstall it.

2
Open the home-screen widget picker

Touch and hold an empty area of the home screen, then choose Widgets. On some Android launchers, you can also long-press the app icon and look for Widgets.

3
Find Weather & Rivers

Scroll the widget list, expand Weather & Rivers, then press and hold the available widget.

4
Place and resize it

Drag it onto the home screen. If resize handles appear, adjust the size to suit your layout.

5
If the widget isn't listed

Open the app once, then check again. If it still doesn't appear, the installed APK may not contain a registered Android widget provider; that requires an app build change, not a phone setting.
